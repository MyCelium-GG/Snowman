# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - unreleased

### Added
- Pack a snowball from snow, push it by walking into it or kick it, and watch it grow as it rolls, pick it up as a Rolled Snowball and stack three into a snowman.
- Snowballs grow without a size limit; the item shows its size, in the inventory and in your hand.
- Icy item names, and every snowball size in the creative menu and search.
- Snowy sounds with subtitles for packing, rolling, growing, stacking, decorating, turning, melting and breaking.
- A craftable Top Hat that keeps a snowman from melting.
- Turn a finished snowman with R; a hint appears when you stand next to one.
- Decorations: coal eyes and buttons, a carrot nose, stick arms, a dyeable knitted Scarf and any item as a hat.
- Snowmen melt where snow golems melt, and drop everything when knocked over.
- Settings for melting, the biggest snowball size, the snow trail and the rotate hint.
- English, German, Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
