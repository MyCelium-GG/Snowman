# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - unreleased

### Added
- Pack a snowball from snow, roll it to make it grow, carry it and stack three into a snowman.
- Decorations from vanilla items: coal eyes and buttons, a carrot nose, stick arms, a carpet scarf and any item as a hat.
- Snowmen melt where snow golems melt, and drop everything when knocked over.
- Settings for melting, the biggest snowball size and the snow trail.
